What these games are, and what they are not

What they are

Each one is built around a single kind of attention — holding one thread while things try to pull you off it, thinking before you move, or not moving at the wrong moment. Every run has an ending. Nothing here is designed to be played forever.

What we do not claim

We do not claim these games improve anything about you. Not your attention span, not your memory, not your performance at school or at work, and not any medical condition. There is no scientific evidence behind this arcade, so there is no claim attached to it. These are games designed around sustained attention, delayed reward and deliberate thinking. That is a description of how they are built, not a promise about what they do to you.

The page for parents says the same thing at more length, along with exactly what is stored and where.

The house rules

  • Nothing leaves your device. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no third-party anything. Your settings and your best run are saved in this browser and nowhere else.
  • No ads and no purchases. There is no shop, no currency, and no way to spend money here at all.
  • No streaks and no timers that call you back. There is no daily anything. If you do not come back tomorrow, nothing is lost, because nothing was ever counting.
  • Every run ends. When it ends the game says so and stops. It never starts the next one for you.
  • Playable with one hand, or one key. Every game works on a keyboard alone, on a touchscreen, and with a gamepad. Motion, screen shake and flashes can all be turned off.

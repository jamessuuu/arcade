House rules

The parents page shows you how to check every one.

Nothing leaves the device
No account, no analytics, no cookie. Settings and best runs stay in this browser.
Nothing to sell
No shop, no currency, no advertising of any kind, including our own.
No streaks or countdowns
Nothing counts the days. Skip tomorrow and nothing is lost, because nothing was counting.
Every run ends
When it ends the game says so and stops. It never starts the next one for you.
One hand, or one key
Keyboard, touch or gamepad. Motion, screen shake and flashes each switch off.

What we do not claim

FTC v. Lumos Labs
settled 5 January 2016

We do not claim these games improve anything about you — not your attention, not your memory, not how you do at school or at work, and not any medical condition. They are designed around sustained attention and deliberate thinking. That describes how they are built, not a promise about what they do to you.

Why that sentence is written so carefully

In January 2016 the US Federal Trade Commission settled with the maker of a well-known cognitive-training product over claims of exactly this kind. The settlement required competent and reliable scientific evidence before any future claim about real-world benefit. We have no such evidence, so we make no such claim, and if this site ever says otherwise that is a mistake and should be reported as one.

The page for parents says the same thing at more length, along with exactly what is stored and where.
